# Marketing Video Scripts

Professional, polished voiceover scripts for short-form video (TikTok, Reels, Shorts).
Each script is 30–45 seconds: hook, three-beat story, and a clear call to action.

## Video 1 — Privacy you can trust (PDF tools)

"Your documents should never leave your device."

**VO:**
"Most online PDF tools upload your files to someone else's server. WiserFiles is different — most conversions happen entirely in your browser, so your documents stay on your device, and anything sent for processing is deleted immediately."

- 0–3s: A locked document with a shield icon over the WiserFiles logo.
- 3–12s: Side-by-side — a generic "uploaded to server" flow vs. WiserFiles "processed locally" flow.
- 12–20s: Quick montage of tools — merge, split, compress, OCR, sign.
- 20–30s: End card with logo and tagline.

**On-screen text:** "Processed in your browser. Nothing stored. Nothing uploaded."

**CTA:** "Try WiserFiles — 25 tools, free, no account required."
